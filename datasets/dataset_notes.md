# CrowdTune Experimental Datasets

CrowdTune was evaluated using publicly available crowd footage obtained from open online sources for research-oriented analytical evaluation.

The experimental scenarios were selected to represent different structural crowd regimes and movement behaviors.

---

## Experimental Scenarios

| Scenario | Regime Interpretation | Source Type |
|---|---|---|
| Shibuya Crossing | Gas-like | Public online footage |
| Akron Marathon | Fluid-like | Public event footage |
| Oasis Concert Compression | Granular-like | Public crowd footage |

---

## Dataset Usage Notes

The videos are used exclusively for:

- crowd dynamics analysis
- instability modeling
- regime interpretation
- forecasting evaluation
- preventive crowd safety research

Raw source footage is not redistributed within this repository.

All rights and ownership remain with the original content creators and publishers.
